Frequently Asked Questions

Answers, before you ask.

What does it mean to sell my house "as-is"?
When you sell your house as-is, you don't need to make any repairs, upgrades, or renovations before closing. As a cash home buyer in Arizona, Ella Lee Homes purchases properties in their current condition — saving you time, money, and stress.
How quickly can I get a cash offer?
You'll receive a fair, no-obligation cash offer within 24 hours of contacting us. Our streamlined process ensures you don't have to wait weeks or months for buyers or negotiations.
Do I have to pay fees or commissions?
No. Unlike traditional real estate transactions, we don't charge commissions or hidden fees. The offer you receive is the amount you'll walk away with at closing.
Can I choose my own closing date?
Yes. We close on your timeline — in as little as 5 days, or up to 60 if you need more time to prepare.
Why sell for cash instead of listing with an agent?
Selling for cash removes the uncertainty of appraisals, financing delays, inspections, and endless showings. With Ella Lee Homes, you skip the hassle and receive your payment quickly — allowing you to focus on your next chapter.
Does Ella Lee Homes only buy distressed properties?
No. We buy homes of all types and conditions — from fixer-uppers to move-in ready properties. We also purchase homes with tenants, vacant homes, homes with financial challenges or pending foreclosure, and even homes currently listed with an agent.
Can I sell to you and still build my next home with Ella Lee?
Absolutely. Many homeowners sell their property to us as their first step toward building their dream home. Because Ella Lee Homes is both a trusted cash home buyer and a leading luxury home builder in Arizona, we can help you transition seamlessly.
