Frequently Asked

The questions investors ask us most.

Direct answers for developers, capital partners, and repeat investor clients.

01 How does pricing work on an Ella Lee home? +

We build on a cost-plus basis: you pay the actual cost of the work plus an agreed builder’s fee, and you can follow costs in your Buildertrend client portal.

02 Why Ella Lee Homes? +

Trust. Knowing that a home might be one of the largest investments you will ever make, we go the extra length to make sure you feel comfortable and secure with that investment.

Our Buildertrend client portal lets you see the schedule, photos and videos, and much more — all from the comfort of your home. Ideal for out-of-state clients.

We believe in ease of mind and ease of process. Many of our clients are investors with repeat projects. The reason: the comfort of having access to their project files from anywhere.

We worked hard to earn their trust. We will work hard to earn yours.

03 What are your services? +

Ella Lee Homes is a custom home builder. We build custom homes in Arizona, including teardown and rebuild projects and new construction on an existing lot.

04 I want to start a project, but I'm not sure where to begin. +

The first step is a conversation. Tell us about your lot and what you want to build, and we'll walk you through how a project runs.

It's highly beneficial to involve your builder early. We can work with your architect, and with our field experience we can flag gaps and inefficiencies and recommend framing methods or materials that reduce costs and improve timelines before plans are finalized.
