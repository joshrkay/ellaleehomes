Frequently asked questions.

Building a custom home is a big decision. Here are straight answers to the questions we hear most — and a direct line when you're ready to talk.

Before You Call

The answers most clients look for first.

Many of our clients research thoroughly before reaching out. If your question isn't covered here, call us at (480) 340-8700 — we're happy to talk it through.

From first sketch to final key, most Ella Lee homes take 14–24 months: roughly 8–12 weeks of design, 4–8 weeks of permitting, and 12–18 months of construction. Timeline depends on scope, lot conditions, and finish selections — we set your specific schedule at contract signing.

A fixed-price contract sets one agreed number up front and is best when the scope is fully defined. A cost-plus contract bills the actual cost of labor and materials plus a transparent builder's fee, which gives you full visibility into spend and flexibility to adjust selections as you go. We'll walk you through which structure fits your project.
