03 What are your services? +

Ella Lee Homes is a design & build construction company. To give clients the easiest streamline for their project, we deliver a full interior finishes design package — choosing materials, finishes, and layouts together.

Our complete design package specifies all the selections and their costs so you'll know exactly what you are getting. We can also engineer the design to meet your budget while honoring your vision of colors, materials, and practicality — all with 3D renderings so you can visualize spaces before giving the green light.

From there, we bring the project to life — turning rendering into reality. We can build new houses or remodel existing ones, add additions to your current structure, or work on commercial buildings. Keeping the whole process in-house gives us more control, making the project go faster and smoother.

04 I want to start a project, but I'm not sure where to begin. +

Typically, the first step is to collaborate with an architect or designer, depending on the scope. Obtaining detailed construction documents is crucial for securing permits and ensuring the project's success.

It's also highly beneficial to involve your builder during planning and development. With our expertise and field experience, we can offer valuable insights to both architect and client — informing design decisions and material selections before plans are finalized.

By engaging us early, we can identify potential gaps or inefficiencies and recommend specific framing methods or materials that reduce costs and improve timelines. Architects and draftsmen are highly skilled, but they don't always have the hands-on field experience that can significantly impact a project's practical outcome.
