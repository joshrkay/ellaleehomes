Ella Lee Homes is a luxury custom home builder serving Arcadia, Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, and the greater Phoenix Valley. We build on your lot or help you find the right one — designing and constructing custom homes from the ground up with full design-build accountability, one team, and complete transparency from first sketch to final key. With dozens of homes delivered across Arizona’s most prestigious neighborhoods, our portfolio speaks for itself.

01
