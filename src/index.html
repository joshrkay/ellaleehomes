Our Story

Ella Lee Homes was born from a simple, heartfelt promise — to build something that would stand the test of time for our daughter, Ella, and the generations that follow.

What began as a dream for our own family grew into a mission to create lasting homes for others. Founder Shay Segev has led every build since the company founded.

Every home we build carries that same devotion — a foundation of love, care, and legacy, designed to nurture life's most meaningful moments for the family who lives in it.

Transparent, Turnkey Process

From permitting to move-in you always know exactly where your project and your budget stand. We build on a cost-plus basis, which means you see the real cost of every line item plus one agreed fee — no hidden markups, no surprise change orders.

Personalized Service

Every Ella Lee home is a one-on-one process built around your lifestyle, your lot, and your vision — never a template. You work directly with the people making the decisions, from first sketch to final walkthrough.

Most Ella Lee homes are complete within 12 to 18 months from the start of construction. Design and permitting typically add three to six months before that, depending on the municipality and whether your lot needs variances. We give you a written schedule before we break ground and update it at every milestone.

Cost-plus means you pay the actual cost of labor and materials, plus one agreed builder's fee. You see every subcontractor bid and every invoice, so there is no hidden markup and no incentive for us to cut corners on quality. It is the most transparent way to build a custom home, and it is how we build every one of ours.

Send us your lot and a rough program and we will give you a real number rather than a range pulled from the air. Cost is driven by lot conditions, square footage, ceiling heights and level of finish, and the difference between those choices is significant. We build a detailed line-item budget before you commit to anything.
