Frequently Asked Questions

Answers, before you ask.

What does it mean to sell my property "as-is"?
When you sell as-is, you don't need to make repairs or upgrades before closing. Ella Lee Homes buys properties in their current condition.
How does the process work?
Tell us about your property by form or phone. We review it ourselves and follow up with a no-obligation offer. If you accept, we handle the paperwork and close on a date that works for both sides.
Can I choose my own closing date?
Tell us what timing works for you and we will talk through what is possible.
Why sell directly instead of listing with an agent?
A direct sale removes the uncertainty of appraisals, financing delays, inspections, and endless showings. It is a private, straightforward alternative to the open market.
What kinds of properties do you buy?
We buy homes and lots in Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, Arcadia, and Phoenix. Contact us to confirm your area.
