Warranty.

Our commitment continues after move-in.

Handing over the keys is not the end of the job. Every Ella Lee home goes through a thorough final walkthrough and punch-list process before you move in, and is covered by a one-year workmanship warranty afterward. We are available long after move-in day.

Before you move in

We walk the finished home with you room by room and write down everything that needs attention — a paint touch-up, a door that needs adjusting, a fixture that is not sitting right. That list is the punch list, and we work through it with you until it is complete.

The workmanship warranty

Your home carries a one-year workmanship warranty from the date of completion, covering defects in the work we performed. If something is not right, tell us and we will come look at it.

Manufacturer and system warranties

Appliances, mechanical systems, roofing, windows, and similar products carry their own manufacturer warranties, which run for their own terms and are registered in your name. Your project documents include the paperwork and registration details for each.

What the workmanship warranty does not cover

  • Normal wear, and the settling, shrinkage, and seasonal movement that every new home goes through.
  • Damage from deferred or missed maintenance — see our homeowner resources.
  • Work, alterations, or additions performed by others after move-in.
  • Damage from misuse, accident, or weather events.

Requesting warranty service

Current and past clients can submit a request through the client portal, or reach us directly at hello@ellaleehomes.com or (480) 340-8700. Tell us what you are seeing and include a photo where it helps — it usually saves a visit.

Licensed in Arizona

Ella Lee Homes is a licensed Arizona contractor, ROC KB2-333410. Work is performed to the standards of the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.
