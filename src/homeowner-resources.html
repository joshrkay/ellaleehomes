Seasonal care in the desert

Arizona is hard on a building in specific, predictable ways: intense sun, a wide daily temperature swing, very dry air, and a monsoon season that arrives all at once. A few habits cover most of it.

  • Before summer. Have the cooling system serviced, and change filters on the schedule the manufacturer sets — more often in dusty conditions.
  • After monsoon. Walk the roof line, scuppers, and drains and clear what the storms moved. Check that water still runs away from the foundation.
  • Twice a year. Look over exterior caulk, grout, and sealed stone. Dry air and movement open small gaps, and sealing them early is far cheaper than the repair that follows.
  • Through the year. Watch irrigation for leaks and broken emitters, and keep planting and drip lines away from the foundation and stucco.
  • First year. Expect fine drywall cracks and small gaps at trim as the house settles. This is normal, and it is worth waiting out the first year before patching.
