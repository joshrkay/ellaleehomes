Our Process

Timeline

Building a custom home in Arizona typically takes 18 months from design through move-in. At Ella Lee Homes, we break that journey into four clear phases, so you always know exactly where your project stands and what comes next. We operate on a cost-plus structure, which means full transparency on every dollar spent, and we build on your lot or assist with land acquisition across Arcadia, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, and the greater Phoenix Valley.

Design Phase
3 – 5 months

It starts with your vision.

Everything starts with a vision. We work alongside you to define the architecture, interior palette, and material selections — translating your goals into a buildable, beautiful plan before a single shovel breaks ground.

Deliverables
  • Architectural drawings
  • Interior design selections
  • Structural & MEP coordination
  • Preliminary project budget
Permitting Phase
3 – 4 months

We take care of the headache.

We handle every city and county submission, coordinate directly with engineers, and navigate HOA approvals on your behalf — so you never have to manage the red tape.

Deliverables
  • City permit submission
  • Engineering sign-off
  • HOA approval coordination
  • Final permit issuance
Construction Phase
12 – 18 months

Let's break ground.

Our crews break ground once permits are in hand. You work directly with the Ella Lee team throughout — no hand-offs, no intermediaries. Timeline depends on scope and finish selections; we set your specific schedule at contract signing.

Deliverables
  • Site preparation & foundation
  • Framing & MEP rough-in
  • Interior & exterior finishes
  • Weekly progress updates
Move-In Phase
1 – 2 months

Welcome home.

We walk every inch of the home with you before keys change hands. Our punch list process is thorough, and our warranty coverage means you're supported long after move-in day.

Deliverables
  • Pre-handoff walkthrough
  • Punch-list completion
  • Final inspection & close-out
  • Ongoing warranty support
Cost-Plus, Fully Open-Book

The cost-plus contract structure is the gold standard for high-end residential construction because it eliminates the hidden margins built into fixed-price building. At Ella Lee Homes, we operate on a fully open-book system — every subcontractor bid, every premium material invoice, and every city permit fee is disclosed with complete transparency. Your budget goes entirely into the physical integrity, custom millwork, and architectural execution of your home, not into a builder’s inflated contingency fund. Our clients track every dollar in real time through their dedicated Buildertrend client portal, from the first design invoice to the final punch-list close-out.
