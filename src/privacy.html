Privacy policy.

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ella Lee Homes LLC (“Ella Lee Homes”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit ellaleehomes.com or contact us through it. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

1. Information you give us

When you fill out a form on this site, call us, or email us, we receive the information you choose to share. This may include:

  • your name, email address, and phone number;
  • your property or lot address;
  • your budget, timing, and details about your project or property;
  • anything else you write in your message.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of each visit. We use this to keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not build advertising profiles of visitors. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy.

3. Services from other companies

Some parts of the site are provided by other companies, which may collect information under their own privacy policies:

  • fonts served by Google Fonts;
  • videos played through YouTube;
  • maps and map tiles from Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, and CARTO;
  • links to our Google, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube pages;
  • our contact form and client portal, which are operated by Buildertrend.

We do not control these services. Please review their policies before using them.

4. How we use your information

  • to reply to your inquiry and talk with you about your project or property;
  • to prepare information you ask for;
  • to serve our clients during and after construction, including warranty requests;
  • to operate, secure, and improve the site;
  • to meet legal and licensing obligations.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other companies for their own marketing.

We share information only:

  • with service providers who help us run our business, such as email, website hosting, form delivery, and project management software, and only as needed for that work;
  • with trade partners and consultants when needed to serve a client’s project;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the public;
  • as part of a sale or transfer of our business, if that ever happens.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. Then we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No website or email system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@ellaleehomes.com or call (480) 340-8700. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep information we are required to keep by law.

9. Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
