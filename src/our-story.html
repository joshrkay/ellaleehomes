Our Story

You dream it,
we build it.

A custom home builder in Arizona.

Ella Lee Homes was born from a simple, heartfelt promise: to build something that would stand the test of time for our daughter, Ella, and the generations that follow. What began as a dream for our own family grew into a mission to create lasting homes for others. Every home we build carries that same devotion: a foundation of love, care, and legacy designed to nurture life’s most meaningful moments.

Today, Ella Lee Homes builds luxury custom homes across Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, Arcadia, and Phoenix. We’ve refined our system for a client-focused experience where every project runs on clear communication, and every client has real-time access to their build through our dedicated Buildertrend client portal, where they can see photos, updates, timelines, and costs all in one place.

Close your eyes and imagine your dream home: a beautifully crafted luxury custom home that balances modern elegance with timeless charm. Nestled in one of Arizona’s most sought-after neighborhoods, your home is surrounded by thoughtful landscaping and architectural details that invite you in before you ever step through the front door.

Inside, natural light fills an open-concept floor plan designed with intention. A gourmet kitchen becomes the heart of the home, while cozy living areas and serene bedrooms offer both functionality and quiet luxury. Whether it’s morning coffee in the sun-drenched kitchen or evenings spent in your private backyard retreat, every square foot is tailored to how you live.
