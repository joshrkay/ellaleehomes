## 3. What we do, and how to say it

| Say this | Never say this |
|---|---|
| Custom home builder. We build custom homes. | Design build. "We design." Anything implying in-house design. |
| Teardown and rebuild, new construction on an existing lot. | Remodel, renovation, addition, kitchen or bath work, commercial. |
| We can work with your architect. | "No architect needed." "All design in-house." Design packages or 3D renderings offered by us. |
| Cost-plus. We build on a cost-plus basis. (Do not use "open books".) | Fixed price, or any wording implying a choice of contract structures. |
| Nothing about services beyond custom homes. | "New homes only." Do not draw attention to what we do not do. |

## 5. Voice

We sound like:

- Sophisticated but simple. Short sentences, plain words.
- Honest and specific. Real numbers, real homes, real names.
- Calm and confident. No hard sell, no urgency tactics.
- A builder talking to an intelligent adult.

We never sound like:

- Condescending or over-explaining.
- A cash-buyer ad. No "financial challenges", no 24-hour offers.
- Marketing filler. No stacked adjectives, no invented awards.
- Over-promising on dates, budgets, or outcomes.

Sell page framing: homes and lots in our markets, a clean and private sale, the next chapter for the property. Shay's line stays, with "lot" added (home or lot).
