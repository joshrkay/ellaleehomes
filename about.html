Our Story

A foundation of love, care, & legacy.

Ella Lee Homes was born from a simple, heartfelt promise — to build something that would stand the test of time for our daughter, Ella, and the generations that follow. What began as a dream for our own family grew into a mission to create lasting homes for others.

Every home we build carries that same devotion: a foundation of love, care, and legacy designed to nurture life’s most meaningful moments.

A design and build construction company.

As a leading design and build construction company in Arizona, we take pride in our expertise in creating distinctive spaces tailored to your unique needs and aesthetic vision.

Together, we’ll navigate construction, architecture, and design to deliver living spaces that embody luxury, functionality, and sustainability.

  • 02 Communicate.Experience seamless communication and full transparency throughout the entire design and build process.
