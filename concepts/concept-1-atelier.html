03 — The Journey

From dirt
to done.

Every Ella Lee home is a collaborative journey from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.

Phase 01

Design

8–12 weeks

Everything starts with a vision. We work alongside you to define the architecture, interior palette, and material selections — translating your goals into a buildable, beautiful plan before a single shovel breaks ground.

Deliverables
  • Architectural drawings
  • Interior design selections
  • Structural & MEP coordination
  • Preliminary project budget
Phase 02

Permitting

4–8 weeks

We handle every city and county submission, coordinate directly with engineers, and navigate HOA approvals on your behalf — so you never have to manage the red tape.

Deliverables
  • City permit submission
  • Engineering sign-off
  • HOA approval coordination
  • Final permit issuance
Phase 03

Construction

9–14 months

Our crews break ground once permits are in hand. You work directly with the Ella Lee team throughout — no hand-offs, no intermediaries. Clear, consistent communication from foundation to final trim.

Deliverables
  • Site preparation & foundation
  • Framing & MEP rough-in
  • Interior & exterior finishes
  • Weekly progress updates
Phase 04

Move In

2–4 weeks

We walk every inch of the home with you before keys change hands. Our punch list process is thorough, and our warranty coverage means you're supported long after move-in day.

Deliverables
  • Final walkthrough & punch list
  • Certificate of occupancy
  • Keys & access transfer
  • 1-year workmanship warranty

Close your eyes and imagine your dream home: a beautifully crafted luxury custom home that balances modern elegance with timeless charm. Nestled in one of Arizona's most sought-after neighborhoods, your home is surrounded by thoughtful landscaping and architectural details that invite you in before you ever step through the front door.
